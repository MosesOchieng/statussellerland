## The visual identity

I'd make the entire website follow your existing app designs:

* **White / off-white foundation**
* StatusSeller green as the primary action colour
* Dark navy typography
* Blue/purple accents for AI/creation features
* Large rounded cards
* Very subtle shadows
* Apple-like spacing
* Real product UI rather than stock photography
* Smooth scrolling animations
* Phone mockups showing the actual seller and customer experiences

Most importantly, **don't fill the landing page with generic illustrations of people using phones**.

The product itself is the visual.

The landing page should make someone think:

> **“Oh. I post my product, someone taps it, the store opens, they can chat, negotiate, pay—and I manage everything from here.”**

That is the "aha" moment StatusSeller needs to own.
